Meetings & Events

Six Spaces, Every Occasion

From a 90-guest gala in the Sunburst Ballroom to a quick one-hour interview in our smallest boardroom, CITY CRUZ HOTEL offers six distinct spaces built around how you actually work and gather — indoors, outdoors, formal or informal.

Grand Ballroom

Sunburst Ballroom

Up to 90 Delegates Floor-to-Ceiling Windows In-House Catering

Our largest and most versatile space, the Sunburst Ballroom takes its name from the arch and sunburst motif in the CITY CRUZ HOTEL crest. Floor-to-ceiling windows and direct kitchen access make it equally suited to a corporate gala, a wedding reception or a full-day conference.

Terrace & Outdoor

The Courtyard Terrace

Open-Air Setting Cocktail Receptions Marquee Option Available

An open-air courtyard beneath the Owerri sky, The Courtyard Terrace is built for cocktail receptions and outdoor celebrations. A marquee can be arranged for larger gatherings or as a precaution against the weather.
